Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a winch if I wheel with friends?

A kinetic tow strap is sufficient for buddy-recovery situations. A winch is essential for solo wheeling or technical terrain where self-recovery is needed.

What's the minimum recovery kit for trail use?

30-ft tow strap, two 3/4" D-ring shackles, and a pair of gloves. Under $100 total. No excuse not to have this in every trail Wrangler.

Synthetic rope vs steel cable?

Synthetic rope is lighter, safer on snapback, and floats in water. Steel cable is cheaper and more abrasion-resistant. Modern synthetic rope is the better choice for most applications.
