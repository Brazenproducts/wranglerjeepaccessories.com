Frequently Asked Questions

Does Bartact make console covers for JK?

Yes. Separate SKUs for JK 2011-18 and JK 2007-10. CJ, YJ, TJ, LJ, JK, and JL consoles have different shapes and are not interchangeable.

Cover vs organizer — what is the difference?

A cover goes over the lid and protects it. An organizer goes inside the storage bin and divides space. You can use both at the same time.

Is the MOLLE option on the Bartact cover worth it?

Yes if you use MOLLE pouches. The panel lets you mount a first aid kit or flashlight holder directly to the console lid.
