Frequently Asked Questions

What tire size can I run on a stock JL?

Up to 33 inches (285/70R17) with minimal to no trimming on most JL trims. Rubicon models have more clearance. 35-inch tires require at least a leveling kit.

All-terrain vs mud-terrain tires?

All-terrain (AT) like BFG KO2 is the right choice for most Wrangler owners who trail occasionally but drive on-road daily. Mud-terrain (MT) tires are louder, wear faster on road, but perform better in deep mud.

How long do BFG KO2 tires last?

50,000-60,000 miles with proper rotation is typical. They are one of the longer-wearing all-terrain tires despite aggressive tread.
