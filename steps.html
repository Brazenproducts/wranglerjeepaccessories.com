Frequently Asked Questions

Do running boards reduce off-road clearance?

Yes, slightly. OE-style running boards typically reduce ground clearance by 1-2 inches at the step. High-clearance nerf bars minimize this. For serious rock crawling, remove them.

Do I need running boards on a lifted Wrangler?

They become more useful as lift height increases. At 3+ inches of lift, many passengers (and pets) will appreciate having a step.
