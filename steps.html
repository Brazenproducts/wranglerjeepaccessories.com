Frequently Asked Questions

Do running boards affect off-road clearance?

Minimally on OE-style boards. Rock sliders are a better choice for serious trail use since they protect rockers rather than just providing a step.

Can I install running boards myself?

Yes. Most JL running boards bolt to factory mounting points with basic hand tools in 60-90 minutes.

OE style vs tube steps?

OE style 6-inch boards look factory-clean. Tube nerf bars are lighter and provide more clearance but less step surface area.
