Frequently Asked Questions

What tire size fits a stock JL?

Up to 33 inches (285/70R17) with minimal or no trimming. 35s require at least a leveling kit.

All-terrain vs mud-terrain?

AT like BFG KO2 for daily-driven trail Wranglers. MT for dedicated mud use — louder and faster wearing on pavement.
