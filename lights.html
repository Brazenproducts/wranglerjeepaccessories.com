Frequently Asked Questions

Spot vs flood beam — which do I need?

Spot throws light far down the trail, good for high-speed dirt roads. Flood lights the area around the vehicle, better for rock crawling. Most light bars combine both.

Do I need a wiring harness?

Yes. Always use a relay-switched harness, not a direct battery connection. Nilight and NAOEVO both include one. Protects your electrical system.

Roof mount vs bumper mount?

Roof gives maximum height and throw distance. Bumper shines at trail level and is better for seeing rocks directly in front. Many builds use both.
