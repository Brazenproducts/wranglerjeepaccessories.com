Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between spot and flood beams?

Spot beams throw light far down the trail — good for high-speed dirt roads. Flood beams light up the area around the vehicle — better for rock crawling. Most light bars combine both (combo beam).

Do I need a wiring harness?

Yes — always use a relay-switched wiring harness. Never run a direct battery connection. Nilight and NAOEVO include one. Protects your Jeep's electrical system.

What size light bar for a Wrangler roof?

40-50 inch bars fit across the roof rack on a JL. 20-30 inch bars mount better to the front bumper. A pod combo kit works on both.
