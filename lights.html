Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between spot and flood beams?

Spot beams throw light far down the trail — good for high-speed dirt roads. Flood/scene beams light up the area around the vehicle — better for rock crawling. Most light bars combine both.

Is Rigid Industries worth the price?

For serious trail runners, yes. Rigid's aluminum housings survive vibration that kills budget light bars within a season. For occasional use, Nilight or NAOEVO offer solid value.

Do I need a wiring harness?

Yes — always use a relay-switched wiring harness, not a direct battery connection. Nilight includes one; Rigid sells separately. Protects your electrical system.
