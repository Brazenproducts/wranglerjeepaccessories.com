Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a winch-ready bumper?

Only if you plan to run a winch. If you're just doing light trails, a standard bumper with D-rings for tow straps is sufficient and much cheaper.

What steel thickness should I look for?

3/16" for the main plate, 3/32" for tubes is typical for mid-range bumpers. Premium bumpers use 1/4" plate. Thicker = heavier but stronger.

Will a bumper void my Jeep warranty?

Bumper installation itself doesn't void warranty. If a bumper causes damage to a covered system, that specific damage may not be covered. Keep documentation.
