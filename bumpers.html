Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a winch-ready bumper?

Only if you plan to run a winch. For light trails, a standard bumper with D-rings for tow straps is sufficient and much cheaper.

What steel thickness should I look for?

3/16 inch for the main plate is typical. Premium bumpers use 1/4 inch plate. Thicker means heavier but stronger.

Will a bumper affect my Jeep warranty?

Bumper installation itself does not void warranty. Specific damage caused by the bumper may not be covered. Keep documentation.
