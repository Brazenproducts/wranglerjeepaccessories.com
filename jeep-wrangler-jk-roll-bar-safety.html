Roll Bar & Safety FAQ

Does Bartact make fire extinguisher mounts for all Wrangler generations?

Yes. Bartact makes a dedicated aluminum mount for the JLU (2018+) and universal padded/MOLLE mounts that fit padded roll bars on JL, JK, TJ, and even UTVs.

What fire extinguisher fits the Bartact roll bar mounts?

Bartact mounts are designed for 2.5-5 lb extinguishers. The Element E50 and E100 are the top pick — no residue, all-class ABC/K, compact cylinder form factor.

What flashlights fit the Bartact roll bar flashlight holder?

The holder accepts 2, 3, and 4 D-cell and C-cell lights. SureFire G2X and Streamlight ProTac both work with appropriate adapters. Check your light diameter against the mount spec.

Why should I have an IFAK in my Jeep?

Off-road driving carries higher injury risk than street driving. A mounted IFAK gives immediate access to a tourniquet and trauma supplies. Bartact IFAK pouches mount directly to roll bars so it is always accessible without taking up cargo space.

Does the Bartact Hi-Lift cover fit both 48 and 60 inch jacks?

Yes — the Bartact jack cover is designed for both Hi-Lift 48 inch and 60 inch versions. The MOLLE exterior lets you attach additional small pouches to the jack itself.
