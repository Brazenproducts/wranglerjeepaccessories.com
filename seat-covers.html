Why is Bartact the #1 pick?

USA-made, consistent quality control, MOLLE webbing for trail organization, and airbag-safe side seams. No other brand checks all four boxes at Bartact's level.

What's the difference between JL and JK seat covers?

JL is 2018+, JK is 2007-2017. The seat shapes and mounting points differ significantly — always verify your generation before ordering.

Are neoprene seat covers fully waterproof?

Yes — neoprene is completely waterproof. Great for Wranglers that get wet frequently. Bartact uses a coated nylon that's also highly water-resistant with faster drying.

Can I install seat covers myself?

Yes. Most seat covers install in 30-60 minutes with no tools. Bartact covers have instructional video support. Amazon alternatives typically include written instructions.
