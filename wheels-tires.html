Frequently Asked Questions

What tire size can I run on stock JL suspension?

Up to 33" with minimal trimming on most trims. Sport models may need trimming sooner. 35" requires at least a leveling kit.

AT vs MT tires?

All-terrain (AT) like BFG KO2 balances on/off-road performance — good for daily drivers who trail occasionally. Mud-terrain (MT) is for serious off-road with reduced on-road manners.

Should I get beadlock wheels?

Only if you plan to air down to 12 PSI or below regularly. At higher pressures, standard wheels are fine. Beadlocks add weight and cost.
