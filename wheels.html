Frequently Asked Questions

What is the bolt pattern for a JL Wrangler?

5x5 (5x127mm). This is also the bolt pattern for JK (2007-2018) and Gladiator JT.

What is the bolt pattern for a TJ or YJ Wrangler?

5x4.5 (5x114.3mm). TJ (1997-2006), LJ (2003-2006), YJ (1987-1995), and CJ all use 5x4.5. This is different from JK/JL.

Can I put JK wheels on a JL?

Yes. Both JK and JL use the same 5x127mm bolt pattern. Most JK wheels will physically fit a JL.
