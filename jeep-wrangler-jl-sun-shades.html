Sun Shades & Bimini Tops FAQ

What makes Bartact tactical sun shades better than mesh alternatives?

Bartact shades are solid-panel, USA-made shades that mount to the hardtop interior. They block far more UV and heat than mesh options, and add optional MOLLE storage strips so the space doubles as storage. Mesh shades like Alien Sunshade work well for soft-top freedom panels but are not interchangeable with hardtop shades.

Are JL and JLU (4-door) sun shades the same?

No. JL 2-door and JLU 4-door have different hardtop panel dimensions. Bartact makes separate part numbers for each — confirm your door count before ordering.

Can I use a sun shade with the factory freedom panels removed?

Hardtop sun shades (like Bartact) mount to the inside of the hardtop and require the top to be present. Mesh shades (Alien Sunshade) are designed for freedom panels and open-air use.

Do Bartact sun shades work with soft tops?

The Bartact tactical sun shades are hardtop-only — they mount to the hardtop interior. For soft tops, mesh shades or bimini tops are the right choice.
