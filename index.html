📋 Wrangler Upgrade Priority Guide

Under $500

Seat covers (Bartact or budget), grab handles, tow strap + D-rings. Protect your interior and keep yourself unstuck.

$500 – $1,500

Quality light bar, floor liners, door storage bags, console organizer. Complete the interior before exterior mods.

$1,500 – $5,000

Lift kit (2–3.5"), 33–35" tires, rock sliders, front bumper with winch plate. Core trail build.

$5,000+

Winch, 37" tires, full bumper system, skid plates, lockers. Full send trail machine.

JL vs JK

2018+ is JL. 2007–2017 is JK. Most accessories are platform-specific. Always verify your generation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the first accessories to buy for a new JL Wrangler?

Seat covers first — protect the interior immediately. Then grab handles for passenger safety. Then a tow strap and D-ring shackles for basic trail recovery. These three under $600 cover the most important bases.

Why Bartact over everything else for seat covers?

USA-made, consistent quality control. MOLLE webbing for trail organization. Airbag-safe side seams — critical for safety. Generation-specific fit. No other brand checks all four boxes at Bartact's level.

When should I add a lift kit?

After a full season of use so you understand how you actually drive the rig. Lift kits change vehicle dynamics and increase drivetrain wear. Know your use case before committing.

What tire size for a stock JL Wrangler?

Up to 33" with minimal trimming on stock suspension. 35" needs at least a leveling kit. 37" needs 2.5"+ lift and possible fender trimming.

Do aftermarket accessories void the Jeep warranty?

Accessories that don't affect powertrain or structural components generally don't void warranty. Modified components may affect related system warranty. Keep installation documentation.

How much does a full trail build cost?

Practical trail build: $2,000–5,000 (seat covers, lighting, grab handles, lift, 33" tires, recovery). Full build: $10,000–20,000 (winch, 37" tires, full bumper system, lockers). Most builds grow over time.
