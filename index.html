Frequently Asked Questions

What accessories should I buy first for a new JL?

Seat covers (Bartact), grab handles (bolt-on only for JL), and a tow strap plus D-rings. These three upgrades under $600 cover the most important bases.

Why does generation matter when buying Wrangler parts?

JL, JK, and TJ have different frame dimensions, bolt patterns, seat shapes, door cutouts, and electrical systems. Parts are not interchangeable across generations unless specifically stated.

Can I use JK wheels on a JL?

Yes — both JK and JL use 5x127mm bolt pattern. Most JK wheels fit a JL.

What tire size for a stock JL?

Up to 33 inches with minimal trimming. 35-inch tires need at least a leveling kit.
