📋 Wrangler Upgrade Priority Guide

Under $500

Seat covers (Bartact or budget), grab handles, tow strap + D-rings. These three protect your interior and keep you unstuck.

$500 – $1,500

Add a quality light bar, floor liners, door storage bags, and a console organizer. Complete the interior before exterior mods.

$1,500 – $5,000

Lift kit (2–3.5"), 33–35" tires, rock sliders, front bumper with winch plate. This is the core trail build.

$5,000+

Winch, 37" tires, full bumper system, skid plates, lockers. Full send — purpose-built trail machine.

JL vs JK Compatibility

2018+ is JL. 2007–2017 is JK. Many accessories are platform-specific. Always verify your generation before ordering.

Bartact vs Amazon

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the first accessories to buy for a new JL Wrangler?

Seat covers first — they protect the interior immediately and are reversible. Then grab handles for passenger safety. Then a tow strap and D-ring shackles for basic trail recovery. These three under $600 cover the most common Wrangler pain points.

Why do you recommend Bartact over everything else for seat covers?

USA-made with consistent quality control. MOLLE webbing for trail organization. Airbag-safe side seam cuts — critical for safety. Generation-specific fit. No other brand checks all four boxes. Amazon options are valid alternatives for budget builds.

When should I add a lift kit?

After a full season of use so you understand how you use the rig. Lift kits change vehicle dynamics and increase wear on drivetrain components. Know your use case before committing.

What tire size for a stock JL Wrangler?

Up to 33" with minimal trimming on stock suspension. 35" requires at minimum a leveling kit. 37" needs 2.5"+ lift and possible fender trimming.

Do aftermarket accessories void the Jeep warranty?

Accessories that don't affect powertrain or structural components generally don't void warranty. Modified components may affect warranty for related systems. Keep installation documentation.

How much does a full Wrangler trail build cost?

Practical trail build: $2,000–5,000 (seat covers, lighting, grab handles, lift, 33" tires, basic recovery). Full-featured build: $10,000–20,000 (winch, 37" tires, full bumper system, lockers). Most builds grow over time.
