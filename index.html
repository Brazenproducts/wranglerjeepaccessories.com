Frequently Asked Questions

What are the first accessories to buy for a new JL Wrangler?

Seat covers first — Bartact if budget allows, FH Group if tight. Then grab handles for passenger safety. Then a tow strap + D-rings for basic recovery. These three under $600 cover the most important trail bases.

Why Bartact for seat covers and grab handles?

USA-made, consistent quality, MOLLE webbing for organization, airbag-safe construction, and generation-specific fit. No other brand checks all boxes. Amazon alternatives are valid for budget builds.

When should I lift my Wrangler?

After a full season of use so you know how you actually drive it. Lift kits change vehicle dynamics and increase drivetrain wear. Know your use case before committing.

What tire size for a stock JL?

Up to 33" with minimal trimming. 35" needs a leveling kit minimum. 37" needs 2.5"+ lift and possible fender trimming.

How much does a complete trail build cost?

Practical trail build: $2,000–5,000 (seat covers, grab handles, lighting, lift, 33" tires, recovery gear). Full featured: $10,000–20,000. Most builds grow one upgrade at a time.
