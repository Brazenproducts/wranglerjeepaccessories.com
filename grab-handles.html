Frequently Asked Questions

Are grab handles really necessary?

Yes for any serious trail use. Factory handles are minimal. Real grab handles let passengers brace during articulation and reduce driver distraction from passenger instability.

What makes Bartact handles different?

The MOLLE webbing built into the handle itself — you can attach pouches, first aid kits, and tools directly to the grab handle. No other brand does this.

Do grab handles fit both JL and JK?

Most handles are platform-specific. Always verify fitment. JL roll bars differ from JK in diameter and mounting point spacing.
