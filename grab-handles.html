Frequently Asked Questions

Are grab handles really necessary?

Yes for any serious trail use. Factory handles are minimal. Real grab handles let passengers brace during articulation and reduce driver distraction from passenger instability.

What makes Bartact handles different?

Hand-woven 550 paracord made in the USA, MOLLE webbing for attaching pouches and gear, and generation-specific bolt-on or wraparound fitment. No other brand combines all of these.

Can I use wraparound grab handles on a JL or JLU?

No. The 2018+ JL and JLU roll bars contain SRS airbags. Wraparound handles from any brand can impede airbag deployment. Always use bolt-on style handles on JL/JLU — Bartact's bolt-on paracord handles are specifically designed for this.

Which Bartact handle for JK vs JL?

For JL/JLU 2018+: Bartact bolt-on paracord handles (airbag-safe). For JK, JKU, TJ, LJ, YJ, CJ: Bartact wraparound roll bar handles. Both are made in the USA from 550 paracord.

Bolt-on vs wrap-around — which is better?

Bolt-on (Bartact JL style) is essential for JL/JLU due to roll bar airbags. Wrap-around is the classic solution for JK and older — fits any 2-4 inch roll bar without tools.
