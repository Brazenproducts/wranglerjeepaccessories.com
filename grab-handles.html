Frequently Asked Questions

Are grab handles really necessary?

Yes for any serious trail use. Factory handles are minimal. Real grab handles let passengers brace during articulation and reduce driver distraction.

What makes Bartact handles different?

MOLLE webbing built into the handle itself. You can attach pouches, first aid kits, and tools directly to the grab handle. No other brand does this.

Do grab handles fit both CJ, YJ, TJ, LJ, JK, and JL?

Most handles are platform-specific. Verify fitment. JL roll bars differ from JK in diameter and mounting point spacing.

Bolt-on vs wrap-around which is better?

Bolt-on (Bartact style) is more secure and cleaner-looking. Wrap-around is easier to install and works on both CJ, YJ, TJ, LJ, JK, and JL.
